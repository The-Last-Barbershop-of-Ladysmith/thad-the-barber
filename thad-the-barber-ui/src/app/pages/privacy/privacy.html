Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

What we collect

When you book with {{ shopName() }} on this site, we ask for your name, your phone number and the time you pick. That's all: there's no account, no email address and no payment.

Why we collect it

Your name and phone book the appointment, so the shop knows who's coming and can reach you about it. We don't sell them, and we don't use them for marketing.

Where it's kept

Bookings go straight into Square, the shop's booking system. This site keeps no copy of its own. If you've booked before, your booking is linked to the customer record Square already has for your phone number. Square's privacy notice covers how Square handles it.

How long it's kept

Your details stay in the shop's Square customer list until the shop removes them. To have your details corrected or deleted, call or text the shop.

The bot check

Before a booking goes through, Google reCAPTCHA checks that you're a person, not a bot. It uses Google cookies and sends information about your device and how you use the page to Google. The Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

The map

The map on the home page comes from Google Maps, which may set Google cookies when it loads.

Site statistics

To find errors and slow pages, the site records which pages are visited, how fast they load and the errors it hits, with Microsoft Azure Application Insights. It uses no cookies and never records names or phone numbers.

Your browser

The site saves the shop's details, such as its hours and address, in your browser so pages load faster. It saves nothing about you or your bookings.

After you book, you get a link to manage your appointment. Anyone with that link can reschedule or cancel it, so keep it to yourself.

Questions

Call or text {{ shopName() }} at {{ phone().display }}. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above it.
